📖 About This Tutorial Series

This comprehensive tutorial series was created specifically for complete beginners who want to learn digital art with Paintstorm Studio. Unlike scattered YouTube videos or dense manuals, this course provides a structured, step-by-step journey from your very first brushstroke to creating complete digital paintings.

Every lesson includes visual examples, interactive exercises, and real-world projects. You'll not only learn HOW to use the tools, but also WHEN and WHY to use them. By the end of this course, you'll have the confidence and skills to create your own digital artwork!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior art experience? ▼
No! This course is designed for complete beginners. We start with the absolute basics and build up gradually. If you can hold a mouse or stylus, you can follow along!
Do I need a graphics tablet? ▼
While a graphics tablet is highly recommended for digital painting, you can start learning with just a mouse. Many concepts and tools can be practiced without pressure sensitivity. However, for best results, consider getting even a basic tablet.
How long will it take to complete the course? ▼
The core lessons can be completed in about 6-8 hours of focused learning. However, mastery comes from practice! We recommend spending at least 30 minutes daily practicing what you learn. Most students feel comfortable with the basics after 2-4 weeks of regular practice.
Is this course free? ▼
Yes! This entire tutorial series is completely free. It was created to help beginners get started with digital art without any barriers. Share it with others who might benefit!
What version of Paintstorm Studio do I need? ▼
These tutorials work with Paintstorm Studio version 2.0 and above. Most features are consistent across versions, though some interface elements might look slightly different in newer versions.
